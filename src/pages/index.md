Welcome! This site is a **fan project** to help **Pokopia** players learn the game. It is not official; you and your family can replace this text with real tips as you play.

## What you will find here

- **[Progression](/progression/getting-started)** — how to move forward early and mid-game (fill in from your experience).
- **[Pokémon](/pokemon/overview)** — notes on creatures, types, and **abilities** (add facts as you discover them).
- **[Habitats](/habitats/basics)** — how you build and improve homes for your team.

## For young builders

Most pages are written in **Markdown** (`.md` files). That is plain text with simple symbols for headings and lists. Open any file under `src/pages/` in an editor and change the words — save, refresh the browser, and your site updates.

When you are ready, a parent can help put the site on the internet for free using the steps in `README.md`.
